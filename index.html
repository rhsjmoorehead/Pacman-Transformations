TRANSFORMATION SYNTAX GUIDE

1. Translations

(x-2, y) Move Left 2 units
(x+2, y) Move Right 2 units
(x, y+4) Move Up 4 units
(x, y-3) Move Down 3 units

2. Rotations

rotate 90 cw 90° Clockwise
rotate 90 ccw 90° Counter-Clockwise
rotate 90 cw about (1,2) Rotate 90° CW around center (1,2) (Level 4)
rotate 180 cw about (0,0) Rotate 180° around center (0,0) (Level 4)

3. Reflections

reflect x=c Reflect across vertical line x=c
reflect y=c Reflect across horizontal line y=c
reflect y=x Reflect across diagonal line y=x
reflect y=-x Reflect across diagonal line y=-x

4. Dilations (Level 4)

2(x,y) Enlarge Ms. Pac-Man to 2x size
0.5(x,y) Shrink Ms. Pac-Man to 0.5x size

GAME RULES & CONSTRAINTS:

  • Left/Right Movement: Bow must be on TOP for all levels.
  • Up/Down Bow Restrictions: Level 1 requires Bow on LEFT to move UP and Bow on RIGHT to move DOWN. Levels 2–4 remove left/right bow requirements for vertical movement.
  • Rotation Rules: Levels 1–3 only allow rotations at Ms. Pac-Man's current location. Level 4 allows rotation about any center point!
  • Reflection Rules: Levels 1–3 require reflection axis to match current x or y position. Level 4 allows any reflection!
  • Dilation (Level 4): Supports dilations k(x,y) where k is between 0.25 and 3.
  • Wall Rules: Rotations and reflections that land on a wall are not allowed.
  • Ghost Rules (Levels 3 & 4): Stepping on normal ghosts causes Game Over. Power pellets turn ghosts purple; stepping on purple ghosts removes them (+100 pts).
